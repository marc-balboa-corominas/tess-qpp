# Project Roadmap

## Completed foundation

### Phase 0 - Baseline reconstruction and audit

Reconstruction, audit, and validation of the effective public-code baseline procedure.

Status: **complete and frozen**.

### Phase 1 - Controlled synthetic benchmarks

Synthetic methodological benchmarks, numerical diagnostics, and nested-window support analysis.

Status: **complete and frozen**.

### Phase 2 - Pilot observational robustness

Prospectively specified observational robustness analysis and documentary synthesis.

Status: **complete and frozen**.

## Literature positioning

### Bibliographic Audit II

Bounded literature audit used to constrain wording, precedence, comparator claims, and the Phase 3 design record.

Status: **complete**.

## Phase 3

### Phase 3A - Catalogue-scale observational robustness

Catalogue-scale implementation of the prospective robustness architecture, including baseline reproduction, methodological perturbations, input admissibility, numerical seed checks, and conditional period robustness.

Status: **complete and frozen**.

Primary freeze: `phase3a-complete-v2`.

### Phase 3B - Synthetic injection-recovery and independent HELDOUT evaluation

Synthetic known-truth evaluation with prospectively separated DEVELOPMENT and single-use HELDOUT evidence. The preregistered candidate-rule gate did not promote a replacement rule, so the untouched AFINO 0.5 10/10 baseline was retained for HELDOUT.

Status: **complete and frozen**.

Primary freeze: `phase3b-complete-v1`.

## Later development

Later phases are reserved for new population and physical-inference work, including any defensible transport or correction layer required to connect the synthetic selection characterization to an observational target population.

These later analyses are outside the frozen scientific scope of Manuscript 1.

## Manuscript 2

A second principal manuscript is reserved for the later population and physical-inference stage of the research program, subject to the evidence produced by those future phases.

## Scientific boundary

The completed Manuscript 1 program provides distinct reproduction, observational-robustness, numerical, and synthetic-ground-truth evidence. It does not establish observational physical validation of AFINO or a validated observational population correction.

## Repository principle

The project repository represents the complete research program and must not be structurally coupled to a single phase or manuscript. Historical protocols, decision records, evidence files, and freezes are preserved rather than rewritten to match the current publication state.
