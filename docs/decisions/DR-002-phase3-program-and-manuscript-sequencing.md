# DR-002 — Phase 3 program structure and manuscript sequencing

**Date:** 2026-08-07
**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Phase 2 closed with:

`PHASE2_COMPLETE_ROBUSTNESS_MANUSCRIPT_VIABLE_CORRECTION_REQUIRES_PHASE3`

The Phase 2 synthesis established that a methodological robustness manuscript could already be defended within explicit limitations.

It did not establish observational validation of AFINO, observational or physical ground truth, sensitivity, specificity, an observational false-positive rate, or a validated correction of the procedure.

Phase 2 also established that the ten F2 events have already been observed analytically and cannot simultaneously be used to develop and independently validate a corrective rule.

## Decision

The project will not prepare the Phase-2-only robustness manuscript at this stage.

Instead, Phase 3 will proceed as two distinct scientific workstreams.

### Phase 3A

Catalogue-scale observational robustness.

Phase 3A will test how the observational robustness phenomena identified in the frozen F2 pilot generalize to a substantially broader, prospectively defined observational cohort.

Phase 3A remains an observational robustness study.

It does not by itself establish physical ground truth, sensitivity, specificity, observational false-positive rate, or validated correction.

### Phase 3B

Realistic injection-recovery and independent held-out validation.

Phase 3B will provide explicit synthetic ground truth and the development/validation separation required for any correction or performance claim.

Any corrective rule evaluated confirmatorily must be defined before access to its held-out validation data.

### Manuscript 01

The first principal manuscript will be considered after both Phase 3A and Phase 3B have reached their scientific closure gates.

Its intended role is to integrate:

- the frozen F0–F2 methodological foundation;
- catalogue-scale observational robustness from F3A;
- injection-recovery and independent validation evidence from F3B.

The existence of a viable F0–F2 robustness manuscript remains historically true but its preparation is deferred.

### Later phases and Manuscript 02

Population correction, physical relations, and later sensitivity analyses remain downstream work.

A second manuscript is reserved for that broader population and physical-inference stage.

## Non-reinterpretation rule

This decision changes project sequencing only.

It does not modify, supersede, or retroactively reinterpret any scientific result, limitation, claim status, or prohibited conclusion frozen at Phase 2 closure.
