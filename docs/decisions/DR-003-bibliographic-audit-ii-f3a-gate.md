# DR-003 — Bibliographic Audit II F3A gate

## Status

**Accepted — gate frozen**

## Date

2026-08-11

## Decision

The Bibliographic Audit II pre-F3A gate is:

`F3A_DESIGN_RECONSIDERATION_REQUIRED`

## Context

BAII.1 froze the literature-search/screening design, BAII.2 froze the raw systematic corpus,
BAII.3 froze work resolution and screening, and BAII.4 froze structured extraction plus descriptive
overlap against the pre-extraction F3 comparison reference.

BAII.4 identified two `DIRECT` F3A overlaps with `F3A_REDRAFT_REQUIRED`:
BAIIW0001 and BAIIW0003, plus thirteen additional `F3A_DESIGN_ADJUSTMENT_POSSIBLE` works.

## Evidence

BAIIW0001 reports catalogue-scale 20-second TESS flare/QPP analysis using AFINO and is independently
sufficient to satisfy the highest preregistered gate branch. BAIIW0003 reports a much larger TESS
flare catalogue with fully convolutional QPP classification and independently confirms that
catalogue scale/TESS/QPP classification cannot define the F3A contribution by themselves.

BAIIW0003 retains a documented source-access limitation. BAII.5 therefore uses only the high-level
catalogue/classification facts already supported by the frozen evidence and does not infer missing
validation details.

The 15 F3A-impact works, 11 comparator candidates and 9 F3B-impact works are represented in the
BAII.5 closure artifacts.

## Consequences

The existing pre-BAII conception of F3A must not be scientifically frozen without prospective
reconsideration of cohort universe, catalogue source, event selection, QPP reference labels,
robustness dimensions, numerical diagnostics, comparator strategy and positioning.

This does **not** mean that F3A is cancelled, invalidated, or shown to be non-novel.

## What changes now

The program advances to a new design task:

`F3A.1 — prospective reconsideration and freeze of the F3A scientific design from the BAII.5 gate`

That task must resolve the open gate requirements before catalogue-scale execution.

## What does not change

- F0–F2 remain frozen.
- BAII.1–BAII.4 remain frozen.
- The BAII systematic denominator remains 190 works / 40 primary extracted works.
- F3A is still not scientifically frozen.
- F3B is still not scientifically frozen.
- No comparator has been adopted by BAII.
- No novelty or priority claim is authorized.

## Next gate

F3A.1 must produce a prospectively frozen F3A scientific design that explicitly addresses the
BAII.5 gate requirements. Scientific execution may begin only after that design freeze.
