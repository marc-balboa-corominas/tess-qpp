The observational inputs are public TESS fast-cadence light curves distributed by MAST. The frozen observational cohort is restricted to TESS Sectors 27–80; the corresponding 20-second light-curve collection is mapped to MAST DOI 10.17909/t9-st5g-3177. Frozen cohort identities, methodological configuration files, and phase-specific provenance are recorded in the public TESS-QPP repository and project OSF record. Synthetic products and held-out summaries are project-generated artifacts within the frozen Phase 3B design and do not constitute an observational population correction.
