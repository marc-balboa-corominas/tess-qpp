Please consider the manuscript “Reproducibility, Methodological Robustness, and Synthetic-Domain Selection Properties of AFINO for TESS Stellar-Flare QPP Analysis” for publication as an Article in The Astrophysical Journal.

The manuscript presents a bounded methodological and synthetic-domain characterization of AFINO in the context of TESS stellar-flare QPP analysis. It combines public-code reproduction, prospectively specified observational robustness tests, numerical diagnostics, a development-only candidate-rule gate, and an independent single-use synthetic HELDOUT evaluation. The interpretation deliberately keeps observational reference labels separate from physical ground truth and does not claim an observational population correction.

The work is submitted as a single-author manuscript. It received no external funding, and the author declares no competing interests. The manuscript and its author-approved figures/tables are derived from frozen, auditable project states; supporting provenance is available through the public project repository and OSF record.

This submission is not under dual-anonymous review. A preprint may be deposited according to the submission plan after final submission-package approval.
