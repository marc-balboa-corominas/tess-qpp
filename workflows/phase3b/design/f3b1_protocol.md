# F3B.1 — Preregistered injection–recovery and development/held-out validation protocol

## 1. Why Phase 3B is required after Phase 3A

Phase 3A characterized how the frozen AFINO-based catalogue classification behaves when observational analysis choices are perturbed at catalogue scale. In particular, an observational event labelled QPP-selected by the source catalogue is a reference classification, not proof that a QPP is physically present, and an event not selected by that procedure is not proof that a QPP is absent. F3B therefore changes the inferential problem. It evaluates the frozen classifier under synthetic conditions where the QPP state is known by construction, while preserving a strict development/held-out separation for any optional rule change. F2 and F3A observations may inform methodological realism and known failure modes, but they are never independent held-out truth. F3B.1 is design only: no synthetic light curve, optimizer execution, scientific result or held-out outcome exists at this freeze.

## 2. Synthetic ground truth

The primary truth states are `SYNTHETIC_QPP_PRESENT` and `SYNTHETIC_QPP_ABSENT`. Positive series have `synthetic_ground_truth_known=true` and contain an explicitly parameterized QPP component with a recorded true period, signal fraction, family and phase under the generator contract. Null series contain the same class of nuisance background and flare envelope but `qpp_component=NONE`. The null state is therefore known because no QPP component is added, not because an observational classifier failed to select an event. Real observational backgrounds are not permitted as the primary null truth population. Observational source labels, F2 labels and F3A roles are also prohibited as truth fields. The positive and null members of a pair share the same nuisance background realization so that classifier changes can be compared under matched nuisance conditions. Truth is used only for evaluation and development scoring; it is prohibited as an inference-time feature. This distinction permits synthetic sensitivity, specificity and FPR while explicitly preventing those quantities from being described as observational performance estimates.

## 3. Simulation domain and realism

The primary domain is prospectively bounded in `f3b1_simulation_domain.csv`. The AFINO in-domain period support is 40–300 s, with the additional admission rule `duration_s / true_period_s >= 3`. The primary cadence is fixed at 20 s. Sampling lengths are 15, 30, 60 and 120 cadences, giving the corresponding validated F1 durations. The primary signal-strength values are QPP fractions 0.01, 0.02 and 0.04 of the flare envelope, while red-noise slopes are 0, 1 and 2 with post-standardization sample standard deviation 0.005. Independent photometric white-noise amplitude is fixed to zero because the bound F1 evidence does not provide a validated range for a separate term; alpha zero remains the white-spectrum Fourier-background case. Flare morphology, peak placement, rise and decay timescales are retained from the validated F1 generator. Two secondary deterministic gap/quality challenges test input admissibility and are never prevalence-weighted as if they represented observed TESS frequencies.

## 4. Generator contract

The generator is frozen before implementation. A native 20-s time grid is created, followed by the asymmetric flare envelope. A power-law Fourier background is generated with the F1 spectral construction, zero DC component, explicit Nyquist handling and per-realization standardization. Positive series add a stationary fixed-frequency sinusoid multiplied by the flare envelope; null series do not. The primary signal family is therefore `STATIONARY_ENVELOPE_MODULATED_SINUSOID`, with no additional damping, chirp or finite-Q parameter. A deterministic quality/gap mask is applied only after the latent flux has been composed. No interpolation or gap filling is permitted. DEVELOPMENT and HELDOUT must use exactly the same generator code and semantics. Background identity deterministically seeds independent noise and QPP-phase random streams, and a failed generation is not silently replaced by a redraw. Any semantic generator change after `phase3b-design-v1` requires a new prospective design freeze, such as `phase3b-design-v2`, before any further synthetic data are generated.

## 5. Development and held-out architecture

The split unit is `background_realization_id`, not an individual injected series. Every series derived from one nuisance realization—including its paired synthetic null, synthetic positive and any preassigned admissibility challenge—remains in exactly one split. The registry contains DEVELOPMENT and HELDOUT identities and their frozen strata, but no synthetic arrays. Assignment is deterministic, stratified and independent of any AFINO result. Within each of 36 `n_samples × red_noise_alpha × qpp_fraction` strata, 100 frozen background identities are ranked by a SHA-256 rule; the first 50 are DEVELOPMENT and the remaining 50 are HELDOUT. The held-out set is single-use. Its noise, periods, phases and flux series cannot be generated before an exact `FINAL_RULE_FREEZE`. If a candidate rule fails held-out validation, the same held-out set cannot be used to tune thresholds or features and cannot be renamed as a new validation sample. A revised method would require a new independent validation dataset and a new prospective freeze.

## 6. Sampling plan and sample-size justification

There are 1,800 background realizations per split: 36 strata multiplied by 50 backgrounds. Each contributes one primary positive and one primary null, providing 1,800 primary positive and 1,800 primary null series per split. A deterministic subset of five backgrounds per stratum and split also receives two gap/quality challenge masks for each truth state. Including these challenge units gives 2,160 planned positives and 2,160 planned nulls per split, or 8,640 planned simulation units overall. The primary AFINO budget is 7,200 decisions and 21,600 model calls because each primary eligible series requires M0, M1 and M2. Sample size was fixed before data using a 95% Wilson-score precision target. Worst-case binomial uncertainty at p=0.5 requires at least 1,533 observations per truth class per split to achieve an absolute half-width no greater than 0.025. The chosen 1,800 gives a worst-case half-width of approximately 0.0231. This precision applies to the overall primary truth class in each split, not to every high-dimensional interaction cell. The computational budget remains comparable to the completed Phase-3A execution.

## 7. Input admissibility

Input admissibility is an evidence plane distinct from classification. Each planned series first receives an input state: `ELIGIBLE_FOR_AFINO` or `INPUT_INADMISSIBLE`. Inadmissible series are never recoded as `NOT_SELECTED`, FN or TN. The primary contiguous-all-good regime is designed to characterize classifier performance under the validated synthetic input geometry. The secondary regimes `ONE_INTERNAL_NONPEAK_SAMPLE_MASKED` and `PEAK_SAMPLE_MASKED` are deterministic structural challenges for the admissibility pathway. They test whether gaps or removal of the flare peak prevent valid analysis under the frozen input rules. Their frequencies are design choices and cannot be interpreted as estimates of TESS data-quality prevalence. It also preserves the lesson from F3A that loss of analyzability is a methodological result in its own right rather than evidence that a physical QPP is absent.

## 8. Classification and end-to-end metrics

For eligible primary synthetic series, TP, FN, TN and FP are defined from known truth and the frozen selection decision. Sensitivity/TPR is `TP/(TP+FN)`, specificity/TNR is `TN/(TN+FP)` and FPR is `FP/(FP+TN)`. Each proportion must report numerator, denominator and a 95% Wilson score interval. Specificity and FPR are computed only from `SYNTHETIC_QPP_ABSENT`; an observational non-selection can never enter those denominators. Pipeline-level quantities are also reported separately: input-admissibility fraction, end-to-end positive recovery fraction and end-to-end null selection fraction. These are not synonyms for conditional sensitivity or specificity. PPV is not primary because positive prevalence is imposed by the simulation design; if shown descriptively it must carry the label `SIMULATION-PREVALENCE-DEPENDENT`. Every stratum must expose its denominator, and structural zero-exposure cells must be reported rather than assigned fabricated rates.

## 9. Period recovery

Period recovery is separate from classification. For an eligible synthetic positive that is selected as a true positive and has a finite in-support recovered M1 period, the protocol records absolute period error in seconds, relative period error and the logarithm of recovered-to-true period ratio. Error summaries are therefore conditional on selected true positives. The all-eligible-positive denominator is retained separately through a period-estimate coverage fraction and explicit missing-period count. A non-selected M1 center is not a recovered period and is never used to impute a period error. No binary “recovered within X percent” criterion is introduced because no such X is required for the primary scientific question; avoiding that threshold prevents a post-hoc success definition. Selected-true-positive error distributions report medians and prespecified empirical 16th and 84th percentiles. Period recovery does not change the confusion-matrix truth state, and good period recovery among selected positives cannot compensate for poor sensitivity among all eligible positive injections.

## 10. Selection-function representation

The primary selection-function representation is frozen as `STRATIFIED_EMPIRICAL`, not a fitted probabilistic model selected after inspecting results. Three functions remain distinct: probability of input eligibility given true and nuisance parameters, probability of selection conditional on eligibility, and end-to-end probability of selection. Positive primary strata are defined by sampling length, red-noise slope and QPP fraction, with true period additionally summarized in three log-Voronoi bins anchored on the frozen F1 periods 50, 80 and 140 s and clipped to 40–300 s. Null strata use sampling length and red-noise slope; the positive-pair QPP-fraction label attached to a matched null is not treated as a physical null parameter and can be pooled for null estimation. DEVELOPMENT and HELDOUT are never pooled for model selection, and quality/gap challenge regimes remain separate from classifier-performance prevalence estimates.

## 11. Frozen AFINO baseline

The mandatory baseline is AFINO package 0.5 at commit `6aceac9518fc8056052807e666da9d0c8bebb010`. The three models are M0 `pow_const`, M1 `pow_const_gauss` and M2 `bpow_const`. The frozen selection rule is `BIC_M0 - BIC_M1 > 10` and `BIC_M2 - BIC_M1 > 10`. Baseline validation is mandatory even if no correction rule is developed. All three model fits must be available before the classification rule is evaluated; execution failures remain explicit rather than being converted into non-selection. The baseline is characterized independently in DEVELOPMENT and, after the final-rule freeze, in single-use HELDOUT. Poor sensitivity or specificity would be a scientific characterization of the baseline under the frozen synthetic domain, not an operational failure of the study. Conversely, successful execution does not establish observational validity. The AFINO optimizer seed used for primary decisions is seed 0; a distinct numerical-stability plane varies optimizer seeds without changing the synthetic flux realization.

## 12. Optional correction-rule policy

A correction is explicitly not mandatory. Candidate development is restricted to DEVELOPMENT and to a low-complexity two-threshold BIC conjunction using only `delta_BIC01` and `delta_BIC21`. Truth variables, true period, QPP amplitude, red-noise parameters, RNG state, source labels, observational roles and HELDOUT-derived information are prohibited as inference-time features. Threshold candidates are generated deterministically from the DEVELOPMENT values plus the baseline threshold. The optimization objective is balanced accuracy with prespecified tie-breakers. Promotion is a separate gate: a candidate must improve DEVELOPMENT balanced accuracy by at least 0.025, have a paired-bootstrap lower confidence bound for that improvement above zero, and remain noninferior within 0.025 for both sensitivity and specificity. If no candidate satisfies that gate, F3B proceeds with the unchanged baseline and `correction_claim=NOT_ESTABLISHED`. If a candidate passes, its exact thresholds, code hash and DEVELOPMENT evidence are frozen before any held-out generation. No feature family can be added retrospectively because it correlates well with DEVELOPMENT truth.

## 13. Comparator policy

F3A.1 left six BAII comparator candidates explicitly deferred to F3B. F3B.1 resolves all six before development. Four upstream or methodologically non-like-for-like approaches are retained as `CITATION_ONLY`: BAIIW0024, BAIIW0147, BAIIW0149 and BAIIW0154. They inform the rationale for known-truth injection–recovery, selection effects or event-detection completeness but do not enter the primary AFINO classifier experiment. BAIIW0098 and BAIIW0156 are recorded as `UNAVAILABLE_WITH_DOCUMENTED_REASON` for F3B v1 because faithful use would require an unfrozen training/retraining and label protocol, and in the latter case the target is additionally upstream flare detection. No comparator is implemented in DEVELOPMENT or as a held-out comparator in this version. It preserves a coherent primary question: characterize and, only if justified, minimally modify the frozen AFINO QPP classification rule under known synthetic truth.

## 14. Numerical stability

Numerical stability remains separate from selection accuracy. A deterministic DEVELOPMENT-only subset contains one background realization from each of the 36 primary strata, chosen by the smallest frozen split-rank hash. Both primary truth members of each selected background are included, giving 72 series. For each series, optimizer seeds 0 through 9 are run with identical synthetic flux; only the external optimizer seed changes. This yields 720 stability decisions and 2,160 model calls, of which seed 0 overlaps the primary baseline and 1,944 model calls are additional. The recorded diagnostics include selection discordance relative to seed 0, BIC and delta-BIC ranges, formal M1 period range when defined, counts of unique parameter payloads, warning calls, bound contacts and convergence statuses. Stable classification does not establish a unique optimizer optimum, and warnings or bound contacts are diagnostics rather than classification evidence.

## 15. Success and failure gates

Three gates are frozen and must not be conflated. `BASELINE_CHARACTERIZATION_SUCCESS` concerns completeness: all planned primary series must be accounted for as eligible or inadmissible, eligible executions must have complete model decisions or explicit execution failures, and all prespecified metric denominators must be produced. It does not require good classification performance. `CANDIDATE_RULE_DEVELOPMENT_SUCCESS` is optional and applies only to a candidate developed under the frozen policy. Failure gives `NOT_PROMOTED_TO_HELDOUT`; no candidate is required for F3B to continue. A successful candidate is frozen before held-out generation. `HELDOUT_VALIDATION_SUCCESS` is single-use. A promoted candidate must satisfy the same prespecified practical improvement and noninferiority logic on held-out data without any retuning. Failure is `HELDOUT_VALIDATION_FAILED`; the thresholds cannot then be modified and retested on the same held-out sample. If the baseline-only branch is used, held-out success means complete independent baseline characterization, not establishment of a correction. A correction claim exists only after both candidate promotion and independent held-out success.

## 16. Interpretive boundaries and next step

F3B uses known synthetic truth, so synthetic-domain sensitivity, specificity and FPR become valid quantities under the exact frozen generator and domain. They are not direct estimates of observational truth, astrophysical QPP prevalence or real-sky predictive value. The fixed flare morphology, fixed noise normalization, absence of an independent photometric white-noise term, stationary primary QPP family and deterministic gap challenges limit external generalization and must remain visible in reporting. F2 and F3A events are not held-out truth and cannot be repurposed as independent validation because their outcomes have already informed the research programme. Numerical stability does not imply optimizer uniqueness. No correction is promised. At the end of F3B.1, only the design is frozen. The next task is F3B.2: implement and validate the frozen generator and materialize DEVELOPMENT only. HELDOUT must remain ungenerated until a later exact `FINAL_RULE_FREEZE`, preserving the independent validation barrier established here.
